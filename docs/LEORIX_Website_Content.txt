0. Voice & writing principles
Write the way the shoe is engineered: precise, confident, unhurried. Trust is earned by
being specific and by under-claiming relative to the proof.
Specifics over superlatives. A number beats an adjective; a method beats a boast.
Short, declarative sentences.
No hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, ultimate, cutting-edge, world-class.
Describe the method as fact; reserve numbers for after measurement.
Indian-confident, globally legible. Engineered, here.

Module 2 — The Problem
Eyebrow: THE GAP Headline: Casual shoes are styled. Performance shoes are over-
built. Almost nothing is both. Body: Lifestyle footwear is usually designed to
look good and engineered as an afterthought. Performance footwear is engineered
for sport, then loaded with tech you don't need for a full day on your feet.
LEORIX sits in the gap: the comfort and stability of a properly engineered shoe,
in something you'd actually wear every day — and the testing to back every word of
that sentence.
Module 3 — The Approach
Eyebrow: HOW WE BUILD Headline: Design-led. Engineering-backed. Measured, not
assumed. Body: Every LEORIX shoe starts as a design and is then engineered
against defined targets: rearfoot stability, controlled midfoot torsion, even
plantar load distribution, and long-duration wear comfort. We set those targets,
build to them, document them component by component, and validate them. Three
pillars:
1. Defined targets — Measurable performance goals are set before a material is
chosen.
2. Built to spec — Construction and materials are selected to hit those targets,
documented part by part.
3. Independently validated — Prototypes are tested at the Footwear Design &
Development Institute (FDDI).

3a. The Approach
Headline: Targets first. Body: Before design, we define what the shoe must do:
measurable targets for rearfoot stability, midfoot torsion control, plantar load
distribution, and comfort across a 6–8 hour day. Every later decision is judged
against these.
3b. The Engagement — what we’re building and validating
Headline: Engineered and tested at FDDI. Body: LEORIX is developed in a
structured engagement with the Footwear Design & Development Institute, across
design, prototyping, and validation. The engagement is contracted to produce:
A validated footwear platform — five men's and five women's variants on one
engineered system.
Full engineering documentation — 3D CAD models, 2D manufacturing drawings, and
material specifications tied to mechanical and functional requirements.
Functional prototypes fabricated across size ranges for laboratory and human
testing.
A durability and mechanical performance assessment — structural integrity and
failure-mode analysis.
A human biomechanical evaluation — plantar pressure distribution, joint
loading, and functional performance across iterations.
Technical documentation supporting our intellectual property filings. Note:
every item above is contracted scope — sayable today. Outcomes are described as what
the testing measures, never as a result we assume.
