01Foundation

Engineered from the sole up

LEORIX makes footwear for people who are on their feet and expect the ground to be handled. We build sole-first — stability, traction, and all-day comfort designed into the part that meets the world, not added on top of it.

Brand statement

A shoe should read the ground and hold it — so you never have to think about your footing again.

Positioning

For anyone who moves through long days on hard surfaces, LEORIX is the footwear engineered where it matters most — at the point of contact. Where other brands decorate the upper and hope the sole keeps up, we start at the ground and design outward. The result is quiet confidence you feel in the first step and still feel in the last.

Understated by choice. The shoe does the talking; the branding stays low.

On the name

LEORIX carries the lion in it — grounded power, precision, mastery of terrain. Treat it as a source of meaning, never a mascot. It shapes how the brand feels: steady, capable, unhurried. It should almost never appear as a literal lion. The strength is in the stance, not the picture.

02The Wordmark

One word, set with weight

The wordmark is LEORIX in Archivo Black, all caps, tightly tracked. It is the primary identifier — stable, planted, and unmistakable. No icon is required; the type is the mark.

Leorix

Primary wordmark — Warm Tan on Forest Green.

Clear space & minimum size

Leorix

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the cap-height of the “L” on all sides. Never let other elements enter this zone. Minimum width: 90px on screen, 22mm in print.

Approved color pairings

Leorix
Tan on Green — primary
Leorix
Green on Tan — secondary
Leorix
Green on Cream — light surfaces
Leorix
Cream on Ink — high contrast

Do

  • Keep the mark all-caps and tightly tracked as drawn.
  • Use approved color pairs only, at full contrast.
  • Give it room — respect the clear space on every side.
  • Let it sit planted and level, like a foot on the ground.

Don't

  • Stretch, condense, outline, or add effects to the letters.
  • Re-set it in another typeface or mixed case.
  • Tilt, arch, or rotate the wordmark.
  • Place it on busy imagery or low-contrast color.

05Voice & Tone

Say less, mean more

LEORIX speaks the way it builds: grounded, precise, and quietly sure. We describe what a shoe does in plain terms, and we trust the wearer to feel the difference. No hype, no exclamation, no borrowed hustle.

Grounded
plain over clever

Concrete language. Real benefits. We name what you'll feel, not what we hope you'll believe.

Precise
specific over sweeping

Numbers and details where they earn trust. We'd rather say one true thing than five vague ones.

Assured
declarative, unhurried

We state things. No hedging, no shouting. Confidence that doesn't need volume.

Warm
human, not clinical

Engineered doesn't mean cold. There's a person on the other side of every line.

A few house rules

Sentence case

Body and headlines in sentence case. Save all-caps for the wordmark, eyebrows, and labels.

No exclamation

If a claim needs a “!”, it isn't strong enough yet. Rewrite until the fact carries it.

Ground language

Lean on the brand's world — footing, contact, grip, standing, stride. Avoid tech jargon and buzzwords.

Lion, lightly

The lion is a feeling, not a headline. Reference power and terrain by implication, never as a slogan.

06In the World

The same brand, everywhere

Consistency is what makes a young brand feel established. Whatever the touchpoint, green grounds it, tan signs it, and the type keeps its two voices. When in doubt, choose the quieter option.

Product pages

Lead with the shoe on green. One Fraunces line to set the feeling, Archivo for the specs. Let the product breathe — generous space, few words.

Packaging

Green box, tan wordmark, cream tissue. The unboxing should feel considered and calm, never loud. Restraint is the premium signal.

Social

Grounded imagery — feet, terrain, contact. Captions short and declarative. Tan reserved for the mark and single accents, not full fills.

Retail & email

Cream surfaces with green type for readability; green blocks for moments of emphasis. One accent per view. Consistent margins everywhere.

The one-line test

Before anything ships, read it back against the brand: does it sound sure without shouting, and does it feel grounded? If yes, it's LEORIX. If it's reaching for hype or leaning on the lion, pull it back to the ground.
